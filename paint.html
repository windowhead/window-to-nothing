Digital Works

The digital space has room for a lot of creation, be it drawing, editing, creating vectors etc.
In that fashion, I picked up a few of those things, one of them being digital drawing, in which I've created various projects.
Recently, I've also started editing images, creating posters and images like the win95 images, where you have to look twice to see all the details.
